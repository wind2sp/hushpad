## Description (English)

```
Ever shared your screen with a password note still open?
Hushpad is a Windows notepad built for exactly those notes.

- There's no Save button. It saves as you type, and on disk every note is AES-256 encrypted.
- The Hushpad window doesn't show up in screenshots, screen recordings, or screen shares. Only you see it.
- Secret notes open hidden, and anything you copy from them is cleared from the clipboard after 30 seconds.
- Ctrl+C keeps formatting. Ctrl+Shift+C copies just the text.
- Keep HTML and JSON in code notes. Ctrl+Alt+J tidies up one-line JSON.
- Drop screenshots (Ctrl+Alt+S) and screen recordings up to 3 minutes (Ctrl+Alt+R) straight into a note.
- From the tray, Ctrl+Alt+M saves whatever's on your clipboard as a note. Ctrl+K searches your notes and commands.

No installer: unzip it and run. Coming soon for Windows 10/11, and a Mac version is in the works.
The app UI comes in Korean, English, Japanese and Simplified Chinese (View › Language). This video shows the Korean UI.

Follow along: https://gdra.mycafe24.com/hushpad/
```
