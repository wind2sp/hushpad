## Product name
Hushpad

## Short description
A notepad that saves as you type and encrypts every note. On Windows, its window stays out of screenshots and screen sharing. Free and open source, with no account and no server.

## Description
Hushpad keeps the things you used to leave in stray Notepad tabs in one quiet place: meeting notes, text you are about to paste somewhere, API keys and JSON, screenshots and short screen recordings.

There is no Save button. Notes are saved as you type, and your notes and open tabs are still there after a restart.

Every note is stored only as a file encrypted with AES-256-GCM. Other apps, search indexers and cloud sync can read the vault folder, but not what is in it. You enter your passphrase once on each PC.

The Hushpad window does not show up in screenshots, screen recordings or screen sharing by other apps. You can keep a note open while you share your screen in a meeting, and the other side will not see it.

Two kinds of notes:
• Rich notes keep formatting when you paste from the web or a document, and hold images, screenshots and short screen recordings.
• Code notes are for HTML, JSON, config values and API keys, with line numbers and syntax colors. They can also tidy up JSON.

Also:
• Text you copy stays out of Windows clipboard history (Win+V) and cloud clipboard.
• Secret notes are covered each time you open them, and the clipboard is cleared 30 seconds after you copy from one.
• Ctrl+K finds any note or command.
• From anywhere: Ctrl+Alt+N new note, Ctrl+Alt+M clipboard to note, Ctrl+Alt+S screenshot.
• Hushpad can lock itself when you step away or lock Windows.
• Put the vault folder in OneDrive or Dropbox to use the same notes on several PCs.
• Interface in Korean, English, Japanese and Simplified Chinese.

No sign-up, no Hushpad server, and nothing about your use is sent anywhere. Your notes live only in the folder you choose.

Hushpad starts quietly in the tray when Windows starts. You can turn this off in Settings › Apps › Startup. Uninstalling the app does not delete the notes in your vault folder.

Free and open source under GPL-3.0-or-later. Source code: https://github.com/wind2sp/hushpad

If you forget your passphrase, no one can recover your notes. Keep it somewhere safe.
